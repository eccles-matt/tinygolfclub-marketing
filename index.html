3Sponsors

Sell holes to sponsors

Each sponsored hole carries the sponsor's banner, built into the game rather than bolted on. Every tap sends a member to their website, so sponsors get visits they can count.

  • A slim banner on their hole that links to their website
  • Their logo mown into the fairway
  • A monthly report of taps and visits

Sample sponsor: Hector's Deli. Tap their banner under the hole.

4How it works

Built in under a week

  1. We play a quick roundWe come out, play your course and take photos of every hole.
  2. You tell us the storiesHole names, descriptions, local quirks and any custom features you want in.
  3. We build itAll 18 holes, your colours and your clubhouse, in less than a week.
  4. We hand it overYour own link, leaderboards switched on, ready for the newsletter.
